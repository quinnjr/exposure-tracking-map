What Are the Risks at the Site?

Current human exposures at the site are under control. The remedy at the Linemaster Switch Site protects human health and the environment because there is currently no exposure to unacceptable concentrations of contaminated groundwater, soil or indoor air. However, in order for the remedy to remain protective, groundwater cleanup goals must be achieved. A vapor mitigation system was installed in 2012 to address the risk of vapor intrusion at a nearby home. Point of use groundwater treatment systems were installed at two homes adjacent to the site to ensure their potable water does not exceed any unacceptable drinking water standards.

The public should avoid contaminated groundwater and coming into direct contact with the soil, surface water, and sediments at the site. EPA sent letters in March 2004 to neighboring residents and town officials announcing its review of the Linemaster Site cleanup. Copies of the fact sheet were at the Woodstock Town Hall and described the five-year review process and how the community could contribute.
